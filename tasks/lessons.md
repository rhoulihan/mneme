
## Capture timing: same rate, not same moment (2026-09-20, Rick)

`Ruling:`-adjacent capture held 6% (7/112) in the ledger that mandated it, while the same
session produced ~106 flags against 113 rulings — matching rate, 6% co-location.

Rick's refinement: **same rate is what matters; same moment is not.** Capturing *close to*
the discovery rather than *at* it is better, not a compromise — it avoids interrupting the
analysis, and the fact is more thorough because the analysis has finished.

Applies to: any "do X at the moment Y happens" instruction. Bind to the rate and a nearby
boundary, not the instant. RC3 in the capture spec said the instant is the worst moment;
this says the near-miss is actively better.
